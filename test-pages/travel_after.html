Travel notes

Make space for the unexpected

Great travel is more than a checklist. Leave room to wander, learn a few local phrases, and support the people who make a place special.

  • Check seasonal conditions and local guidance
  • Choose locally owned tours and stays where possible
  • Keep plans flexible and travel respectfully
  • Save offline maps and key reservation details
