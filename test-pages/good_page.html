Food with a neighbourhood spirit

GreenLeaf began with a simple idea: good food should bring people together. We work with nearby growers, bake in small batches and make room for everyone at our tables.

Our menu changes with the seasons. We keep familiar favourites while introducing new dishes inspired by the ingredients available each week.

We believe every meal is an opportunity to care for our community.
